About

Why SignMed exists

Many people with hearing and speech disabilities rely on sign language, yet medical professionals often lack the training to understand it. This creates dangerous barriers during emergencies.

SignMed bridges that gap — translating signs in real-time so doctors, nurses, and first responders can understand patients instantly.
